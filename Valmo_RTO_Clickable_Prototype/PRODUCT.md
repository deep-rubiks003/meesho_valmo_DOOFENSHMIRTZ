# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript, chosen after the user confirmed the directly openable format.

## Users

Inferred from the user's request and challenge materials: case reviewers and operations stakeholders exploring how a parcel-exception workflow could work across dispatch, riders, customers, and sellers.

## Product Purpose

An interactive case prototype that demonstrates address and hub validation, customer/rider delivery recovery, refused-parcel disposition, and the associated scenario economics and pilot measures.

## Positioning

One synthetic parcel can be followed through the full exception path, with editable assumptions and visible decision rules connecting each action to its next state and cost.

## Operating Context

This is a demonstration for an innovation case. The prototype is not connected to Valmo or Meesho systems. Parcel records, addresses, hub names, operational rules, and modeled values are synthetic or illustrative unless cited as sourced facts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Screenshot-inspired parcel-exception operations dashboard with four queues, searchable records, owner/action/deadline detail, event history, handoff flow, minimum event architecture, and proposed process metrics.
- Clickable operator views for address confidence, hub mismatch, attempt/contact recovery, and COD refusal disposition.
- Transparent calculators for scenario delivery/RTO counts, partial network costs, refusal recovery, and two-arm proportion sample size.
- Editable rule thresholds and simulated state transitions.
- No live customer personal data, carrier APIs, maps, messaging, GPS, authentication, or backend persistence.
- The prototype must distinguish challenge inputs, external reference facts, synthetic examples, and user-adjustable assumptions.

## Brand Commitments

Use the user's existing case-deck visual cues as a reference: warm cream, deep plum, and orange. Keep the interface readable and suited to operations work.

## Evidence on Hand

The project includes `Valmo_RTO_Solution_Research.md`, `Valmo_RTO_Solution_Research_and_Implementation_Blueprint.md`, `S3_Valmo_RTO_Case_Approach.md`, and `S2_Roorkee_Sapiens_Learnings.md`. No live Valmo order data or primary interview dataset is present. Do not fabricate either.

## Product Principles

- Every exception has an owner, next action, and deadline.
- No response is not a refusal; an uncertain attempt signal is not proof of misconduct.
- Customer choice and rider fairness remain visible alongside delivery/cost outcomes.
- Every displayed calculation shows its formula and exclusions.
- Seller authorization and parcel eligibility are required before local resale.
